So You Don't Have To Studio

A private tool for the YouTube channel @SoYouDontHaveToTV.

So You Don't Have To publishes fact-checked, animated explainer videos about the human body and health science. This tool runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to manage that one channel: uploading the channel's own videos with titles, descriptions and thumbnails, answering and moderating comments, and reading the channel's own YouTube Analytics to make better videos.

The tool is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account.
